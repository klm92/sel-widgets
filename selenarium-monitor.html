Anesthetic Depth Signs
Too Light
Strong jaw tone · eye central · swallowing · purposeful movement · lacrimation · brisk palpebral reflex
Surgical ✦
Jaw relaxed · eye ventromedial · slow or absent palpebral · slow regular breathing · no response to stimulus · MM pink
Too Deep
Eye central again · dilated pupils · flaccid jaw · very slow or absent breathing · pale MM · hypotension · no reflexes
Recovery
Swallowing returns · palpebral returns · jaw tone returns · do not extubate until swallowing confirmed

Emergency Interventions
APNEA
Manually ventilate 8–12 bpm · notify DVM · check depth (too deep?) · have reversal agents ready and labeled
BRADY
Notify DVM · atropine 0.02–0.04 mg/kg IV or glycopyrrolate 0.005–0.01 mg/kg IV · if on dex → atipamezole
HYPOTEN.
Reduce vaporizer · IV fluid bolus (5–10 mL/kg) · dopamine 5–15 mcg/kg/min CRI · notify DVM immediately
HYPOTHERM.
Warm IV fluids · warm air blanket · circulating water pad · cover non-surgical areas · check temp every 10 min
CPA
Call for help · begin CPR · stop anesthesia · epi 0.01 mg/kg IV · follow RECOVER guidelines
LARYNGO.
Cats: lidocaine on larynx before intubation · wait 30–60 sec · O₂ by mask · do not force the tube
